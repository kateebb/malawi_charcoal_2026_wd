Replication package for "Do coupled population, economic, and land use dynamics explain household energy transitions in Malawi?" [World Development, 2026]

All scripts used to process and analyze Malawi's Integrated Household Survey Waves II-V (IHS) and the Global Forest Cover Data v1.9 (GFCD) are included for transparency and replicability. We do not have permission to redistribute source files from the IHS and GFCD, so our replication dataset is a processed and integrated dataset from both original datasets.

Scripts 0a, 0b, 1a, 1b, and 2 are included for replicability of data preparation. 

Scripts 3a, 3b, 3c, 4, and 5 may be used to recreate analyses. These scripts also include code to create Figures 2-5 and Tables 1,2, and 3 in the main text. These scripts also include code to recreate Tables A2, A3, A4, and Figure A1 in the Appendix.
